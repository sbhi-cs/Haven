# Shoot the Sky
### Haven Submission

## What is this game?
* Shoot the sky is a simple bullet hell game
* You dodge bullets and shoot enemies, while ascending farther up

## How to play?
* This game is a web build on itch.io
* You can play it here: <https://rapidblaz3.itch.io/haven>

### Controls
* WASD/Arrow keys for movement
* J/Z to shoot and res
* Click buttons to activate them

### Supplementary details
* You have 3 lives
* After losing your first and second lives, you are prompted to spam "J" 10 times
  * If you successfully do that, you "bounce back" and have another chance
  * Otherwise, you lose
* On your third life lost, you lose
